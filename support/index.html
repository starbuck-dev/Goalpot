Frequently asked questions

How do I add a goal or a debt?

On the Plan tab, tap Add a goal or Add a debt (or +). A goal needs a name, the target and what you have saved so far; add a contribution — per week, fortnight, month or payday — and, if you like, a target date. A debt needs the balance, the APR, the minimum payment and the due day. Save, and the row shows the month you get there; the two dates above the list, Debt-free and Goals funded, move with it.

What is free, and what doesn't count?

Free, for good: up to three active goals and three active debts, counted separately, with every strategy, Compare, the Extra per month dial, one-time amounts, the log with undo, Fast forward, 0 % periods, the chart and the full schedule, CSV export, the share image, widgets, reminders and backups. Reached goals and closed debts don't count, and the Plan tab shows how many you have used before you add one (“Free: 2 of 3 goals · 3 of 3 debts”).

The one-time purchase Unlock everything adds unlimited goals and debts, several plans — for example personal and household, in Settings › Plan › Plans — and future features at no extra cost. Nothing you entered or exported is ever locked. There is no subscription, no trial and no ads; the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

How are the dates and the interest worked out?

Month by month. Once a month, interest is added to each balance at its APR divided by twelve; a payment covers that month's interest first, then the balance. The money for your debts is the sum of their minimum payments plus Extra per month, and it goes to the debts in the order you chose; when a debt is gone, its payment rolls on to the next one. Goals get their contribution until the target is reached.

Every projection assumes that your rates and minimum payments stay as they are today, and Goalpot shows that basis next to it (“vs minimum payments, at current rates”). Lenders usually work interest out day by day, so their figures can differ a little. If a payment doesn't cover the interest, the row says that it never pays off at this payment instead of showing a date.

Calculations are estimates for planning purposes and are not financial advice. Actual interest and dates depend on your lender's terms.

Why does Goalpot compare strategies instead of choosing one?

Because the order is your decision. Compare puts snowball (smallest balance first), avalanche (highest APR first) and your own custom order side by side in two equal columns: the debt-free month, the interest, the first debt gone and the order. The line under them states the difference and nothing more. What do these mean? explains the orders. The same goes for goals and debts: Compare shows what moving part of a payment from a debt to a goal changes on both sides, and leaves the choice to you.

Why doesn't Goalpot connect to my bank?

Because your plan should stay yours. Goalpot never asks for a bank login, has no account and sends nothing anywhere. You type the balance, the rate and the minimum from your statement and log payments and contributions as you make them. Adjust balance brings a row in line with what your lender or your savings account shows: the difference is written as an adjustment, so the history stays. Goalpot doesn't move money and never contacts your lenders.

How do 0 % periods and percentage minimums work?

For a card with an interest-free offer, add a 0 % period to the debt: the date it ends and the rate after it. Until that date no interest is added; after it, the rate you entered applies, and Goalpot can remind you 14 and 3 days before the period ends. A minimum payment can be a fixed amount or a share of the balance with a floor, the greater of the two. In the projections every minimum stays at today's amount, so the money you planned for your debts doesn't shrink as the balances fall.

How do I log a payment or a one-time amount?

Open a goal or a debt and tap Log. Choose the minimum, the plan's amount or your own, the date and the kind — regular, extra this month or one-time — and the sheet shows what it does to the date before you save. Every entry can be changed or deleted in History, with Undo. A one-time amount for a later month, such as a bonus or a tax refund, goes into History › Plan a one-time amount; Skip this month shows what a paused month costs. Fast forward catches the plan up to today after a while away, without entering each payment.

Where are my backups, and how do I restore one?

In Settings › Data › Backups. Goalpot makes a backup on your iPhone once a day after you change something, and before a restore or Delete All Data; the last ten automatic versions are kept. Back up now makes a version of your own, which is never removed automatically, and Share saves a version as a file. To restore, choose a version or Restore from file…: you first see what is in the backup, and Merge into my data adds it. Restoring always merges: nothing is deleted or overwritten, and your current data is backed up first.

Why didn't a reminder arrive?

Reminders are local notifications from your iPhone: on each debt's due day (Payment day), 14 and 3 days before a 0 % period ends, and on payday with what your goals get. Turn them on in Settings › Reminders, and check that notifications for Goalpot are allowed in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications (Settings › Reminders shows Open Settings when they are off) and that a Focus doesn't silence them. The payday reminder needs a payday in Settings › Plan. Goalpot plans the reminders from the dates you entered each time you open it and doesn't run in the background, so open it now and then to keep the next ones planned.

What do the widgets show?

On the Home Screen, the small widget shows your debt-free month with its bar and opens Log with one tap, and the medium widget shows the next two dates. On the Lock Screen, a gauge shows how far one row has come (choose it in Settings › Widget), and a line shows your debt-free month and what is left.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the Apple Account that bought Unlock everything, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings › Purchase or on the unlock screen. The purchase belongs to that Apple Account and works on every iPhone you use with it; it isn't shared through Family Sharing.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings › Data › Export gives Export plan as CSV (every goal and debt with its date) and Export ledger as CSV (every entry) for a spreadsheet app, and Share “debt-free by” image. Delete All Data removes every goal, debt and entry after a last backup; your purchase, settings and backups stay until you delete them. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. We hold no copy, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Goalpot runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later, in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Russian. Amounts and dates follow your iPhone's region. It works fully offline.
